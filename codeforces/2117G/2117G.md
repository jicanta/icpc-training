Very standard and well known idea.
Sort the edges by increasing weight.
Once 1 and n are in the same component, we can freely move and "catch" the minimum weight edge.
Hence, we just process the edges in increasing order by weight and once 1 and n are connected, we update our answer to be the minimum of it and the current weight of the edge and minimum value of the edge in the component of 1.
Every time you have min max of weight edges, its useful to process them in order or sth.
